While travelling, many places are often overlooked by the popular travel areas, which leaves the smaller areas unknown. This site will provide the Top 10 list of unique waterfront places that offer users concise information, visuals, and insights into the area that could be interesting to visit. Allowing travelers to discover, plan, and foresee the trip easily with this simple-to-use website. 
The target users would be individuals, families, and companies

Possible benefits of the project
List the benefits that you foresee the project will bring to the intended user base.

This website offers several benefits to its intended users. It allows travelers to discover lesser-known waterfront destinations that are often overlooked by popular travel sites. By providing concise information, visuals, and key insights in one location, the website makes trip planning quicker. The Top 10 list format makes it easy to find these lesser-known destinations. Visual content helps users understand each area better and make more confident travel decisions. Families can use the site to find relaxing and engaging destinations suitable for all ages, while companies may find waterfront locations for retreats or group travel. By bringing these lesser-known locations to light it will also support local tourism and encourage economic growth. Overall, the simple and user-friendly design helps travelers easily plan, explore, and feel confident about their travel experiences.

Current/similar Solutions
There are several sites like this one, but our site brings in something different in the form of a top 10 list showcasing unique waterfront destinations with information and visuals. This is more of a niche website than a top 10 best places to travel. If we had to note the sites that would be the closest to our site it would probably be sites like Curious and Wondrous Travel Destinations - Atlas Obscura or World of Waterfalls - Explore The World One Waterfall At A Time

Estimated duration of the project
This should take about 4-8 weeks at most to get it up and running.

UI/UX and User-Centric aspects of the project
•	The site will focus mainly on being clear and easy to navigate, with a clean layout and good information to read. The visuals will support information without overwhelming the people, and the Top 10 format will help find things quickly.
•	Depending on how the project develops we might look into adding different versions of list based on basic user personas like solo, family, company which could help with how we organize pages. 
•	We will also try and anticipate how users might try to use the site. For examples we could have a search bar for people looking for specific results quickly or have filters or a comparing type of tool.
•	To check the design of our site well collectively make a rubric and test it amongst ourselves. After that we will get some outside participants to go through the site and get feedback from them which we will use to make improvements. 
•	For accessibility we will try to have alternative text for images. We will also use clear fonts and colors and try to make sure the site works across different devices. The layout will also follow a consistent theme across the pages.
